AI in School and Work

    Artificial Intelligence has greatly influenced how Information Technology is used in both school and work. As a student at the University of the East and a freelance FiveM mods editor, I experience firsthand how AI supports learning and creative work. AI tools help make tasks faster, more efficient, and more accessible. However, they also change how skills are developed and applied. This blog discusses how AI affects IT through my personal experiences in school and freelance work.

    In school, AI plays a helpful role in understanding IT-related subjects. As a UE student, I use AI to clarify lessons, review code, and organize ideas for schoolwork. It helps me learn faster, especially when dealing with programming concepts and technical explanations. Still, I make sure to balance AI use with my own thinking to avoid becoming too dependent on it.

    As a freelance FiveM mods editor, AI has become a useful assistant in my work. It helps with script troubleshooting, optimization ideas, and documentation for mods. Editing and improving mods has become more efficient because AI can quickly point out errors or suggest improvements. However, creativity and actual coding skills are still important, since AI cannot fully replace hands-on experience and game-specific knowledge.

    Overall, AI has a strong impact on IT in both school and work. It increases productivity and supports learning, but it also requires responsibility and proper use. For students and freelancers like me, AI should be a tool that enhances skills, not replaces effort and understanding.
