My Life Story

Childhood Teenage College

Childhood

Where my journey began and values were formed.

I was born and raised in Rosario, Pasig City, where my childhood was full of simple but happy moments. Growing up as the oldest child taught me how to be responsible early in life. I spent most of my days playing with neighborhood kids and enjoying the outdoors. When I wasn’t outside, I played with Legos and built small creations that made me feel imaginative.

My parents always supported me and made me feel safe at home. I learned a lot of values from them and from the experiences I had as a kid. My siblings also looked up to me, so I tried to be a good example for them. Our family gatherings were always fun and full of stories. I loved listening to older relatives share lessons about life. These moments shaped my personality and helped me appreciate the people around me. My childhood may have been simple, but it was full of love, lessons, and memories. Because of that, it became the foundation of who I am today.

Teenage Years

Curiosity and exploration shaped my interests and skills.

My teenage years helped me discover more about myself and what I wanted to learn. In high school, I met friends who supported me and shared the same interests. This was also when I became more curious about technology. I started exploring things like Photoshop, Blender, and After Effects. I also got interested in FiveM modding and tried learning basic programming to improve my projects.

I enjoyed experimenting with different tools and seeing what I could create. Even though school became harder, I learned how to manage my time better. I studied, worked on my hobbies, and still tried to be a responsible older sibling. My friends also became a big part of my teenage life, teaching me loyalty and trust. Every challenge I faced during this time helped me grow. Looking back, my teenage years were important because they guided me toward the interests I still have today.

College

Growth, independence, and preparation for the future.

My college experience at the University of the East has been full of learning and personal growth. I became more independent and responsible with my time. College taught me how to balance schoolwork, projects, and personal hobbies. I continued improving my skills in programming and digital media through assignments and personal experiments. Working with classmates on group projects helped me learn teamwork and communication.

There were times when deadlines felt overwhelming, but they made me stronger and more disciplined. I also joined school activities and met people who shared my passions. My professors encouraged me to think more critically and to be creative. I used school resources like labs and libraries to practice my skills. My family continued to motivate me, especially during stressful moments. College also gave me opportunities to think about my future career. Overall, my time in college helped me grow as a student and as a person, preparing me for the next steps in life.
